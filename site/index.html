Questions, answered

Do my guests need an app or account?

No. The invitation is a web page — it opens in any browser on any phone, tablet or computer. Guests RSVP directly inside it.

How do I send it?

You get one link. Paste it into WhatsApp, SMS, email, or a Facebook group — anywhere you can share a link, you can send a Fira.

Can I change the invitation after sending it?

Yes. Edits publish instantly to the same link, so every guest always sees the latest version.

Where do RSVPs go?

To your private host dashboard — a secret link only you have. You'll see who's coming, party sizes, answers to your questions, and messages.

What does "free while in beta" mean?

All features are free right now. When we introduce paid plans, invitations created during the beta stay free forever.
